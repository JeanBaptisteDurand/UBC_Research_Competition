# The opposing thesis: "Pump.fun: The long degen bet" (Cornell, 2025 finalist)

> Source: the finalist paper (local PDF path [removed for publication: local file path]), Max Tang, Paula Hirsch, Micah Killmer (Cornell Blockchain Club), track **Long > $250M**, written ~22/11/2025. Finalist, did not win (Morpho won the track).
> Read in full on 21/10/2026. **This paper is our "consensus" to refute**: it formulates exactly the bull case the market is paying for today.

## 1. What they claimed (Nov. 2025)
- **Target: $0.0152 at 1 year (Nov. 2026)**; scenarios bear −21% (≈ $0.0021), base ×5.7, bull ×13 ($0.0345). Base = 50/50 average of bear and bull.
- **Valuation**: FDV $2.675B, annualized fees > $870M → **FDV/fees 3.07x**; multiples applied 15x / 20x / 25x (compared with Aave 30x, Syrup 20x).
- **Pillar 1 — social moat**: "Pump captures **85 % of memecoin launchpad volume and > 85 % of all memecoin deployment**"; launchpads are a network good.
- **Pillar 2 — livestreaming**: differentiation against Twitch/Kick; +115% of creators after a viral event in Sept. 2025.
- **Pillar 3 — adaptation**: Pump lost share to BONK in July 2025, then reacted (buybacks, dynamic fees "Project Ascend", creator rewards).
- **Pillar 4 — buybacks**: "**around 100 % of Pump's revenue is used for buybacks**" → any growth in fees = appreciation for holders.
- **Reading of the decline**: "the current drop in activity and fees is due to **macro cyclical headwinds** instead of product incompetence"; the bear case assumes "another bear market… **highly unlikely**".
- **Their own risks**: memecoin fatigue (Solidus: < 0.4% of users with > $10k profit; 99 graduations on 21/11/2025 against 399 at the 08/05/2025 peak), new entrants (BONK), **end of discretionary buybacks**.
- Sources: Dune, Messari, Artemis (custom charts).
